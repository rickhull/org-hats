# org-hats v1 — what remains

**Status:** `1.2.0` is released on `main`: the 1.1 shape plus the additive
consumer addenda — the canonical verification-key set in `org-hats/access`
and the `amr`/`auth_time` authentication claims. The repository work is
done. What remains is consumer adoption and decisions owned elsewhere; the
v0.4 line is frozen at its tags, and history is in commit messages.

## C1 — Consumer adoption

- [ ] **Quartermaster adopts the runtime and retires the stand-ins.** The
  `v1.2.0` pin, the `verificationKeysFrom` swap, and the re-recorded
  acceptance are complete on their side (2026-10-24, relay); the 1.1
  `Organization` field rename rode along with the pin move. What remains
  is theirs: deploy the Cloudflare runtime — pinning the Cloudflare
  revision independently of the portable tag — swap the `AUTHORITY`
  service binding, and delete `apps/authority-dev` plus `packages/access`
  together.

## Open decisions

- The Cloudflare package's release tag scheme (for example
  `cloudflare-v0.x.y`), kept distinct from the root `vX.Y.Z` so
  `bin/check-version` stays unambiguous.
- Production access lifetime and fresh-auth policy: the consumer's
  security review owns the number; the engine defaults to 900 seconds.
- The exchange-material shape for identity adapters when provider secrets
  are sealed inside the Cloudflare runtime.
- A read-only hat is not expressible in v1: `validatePolicyBundle` rejects
  a role with zero actions, so an auditor (read reach, no act capability)
  has no natural carrier. It arrives with the team-audience axis —
  Quartermaster's `read_audience` requirement — not by weakening the
  bundle validation ahead of the consumer that needs it.
