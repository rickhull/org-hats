From actor to access

What does it take for a person to act, and what survives when their identity link is removed?

Follow a synthetic person from an actor record to a linked identity, a hat grant and an issued envelope. The provider evidence is prepared for this lesson. Activation links an identity; it grants no authority by itself. Everything runs in this browser and resets when you leave.

The sequence

Build access, then remove the link

Preparing the lesson…

  1. Create the actor

    Dana, an organization administrator, creates the record and checks access. Without an identity link, issuance is unauthenticated.

  2. Activate the identity

    Dana requests activation; the new teammate accepts it with the prepared synthetic evidence. The lesson then checks access. Without a hat, a link alone does not admit a person.

  3. Grant a field hat

    Len, an operation administrator, grants the hat at Yard crew and checks whether an envelope can be issued. A known actor can be granted a hat before their identity is linked.

  4. Unlink the identity

    Dana unlinks the principal. The actor, grant and issued envelope remain. Try a refresh or a new access request to see the effect of removing the link.

  5. Advance to expiry

    Verify the retained envelope again at its expiry.

Current state

What exists now?

The model clock is paused. “Advance to expiry” moves time; starting fresh resets it.
